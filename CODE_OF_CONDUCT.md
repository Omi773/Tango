# Code of conduct

Be kind and respectful. This project is for people who enjoy music and building things.

- Be patient with beginners and with each other
- Disagree about ideas, not people
- No harassment, hate speech or personal attacks
- Respect other people's privacy

Maintainers may remove comments, issues or contributions that break these rules and may block repeat offenders. Report a problem through <https://github.com/Omi773>.
