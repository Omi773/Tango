# Tango Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 8, 2026_

## Summary

Tango does not run any servers and does not collect, sell, or share your personal data. Everything stays on your device unless you turn on a feature below that talks to another service.

## What stays on your device

- Your music files and library information
- Listening statistics, playlists, queue and app settings
- Network storage connection details, if you add a network location
- If you connect Discord: your Discord access and refresh tokens, stored in the iOS Keychain

## Features that contact other services

These only happen when you use or turn on the feature. Tango has no account system of its own.

- **Update check (off by default).** If you turn on "check for updates", Tango asks GitHub's public releases API whether a newer version exists. GitHub receives the request like any web request (for example your IP address).
- **Lyrics and metadata lookups.** Features that look up lyrics or metadata online send the track's title, artist and album to the provider that performs the lookup. Providers and extensions are described by the extension itself and by that provider's own privacy policy.
- **Discord (optional).** If you connect Discord, the app requests only the permissions shown on Discord's approval screen and uses them to show what you're listening to as your Discord activity. Data sent to Discord is handled under Discord's Privacy Policy. You can disconnect in the app or revoke access in Discord under Settings → Authorized Apps.
- **Network storage (optional).** Tango connects to the servers and shares you add, on your local network or elsewhere, to browse and play your music. iOS asks for Local Network permission for this.
- **Links you open.** Opening a link (for example this policy or a project page) uses your browser or an in-app browser view, which is governed by that site's own policy.

## Permissions

- **Local Network:** to connect to your music servers and network storage.
- **Photo Library:** to save album artwork when you ask the app to.

## Third parties

The app has no analytics, advertising, or tracking.

## Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.
