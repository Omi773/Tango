LocalPlayer Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

Summary

LocalPlayer does not run any servers and does not collect, sell, or share your personal data. Everything stays on your device.

What stays on your device

  • Your music files and library information
  • Listening statistics and app settings
  • If you connect Discord: your Discord access and refresh tokens, stored in the iOS Keychain

Discord

If you choose to connect Discord, the app requests only the permissions shown on Discord’s approval screen. It uses them to show what you’re listening to as your Discord activity. Data sent to Discord is handled under Discord’s Privacy Policy. You can disconnect in the app or revoke access in Discord under Settings → Authorized Apps.

Third parties

The app has no analytics, advertising, or tracking. If metadata or lyrics extensions are used, any requests they make are described by the extension itself.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.
